# To dowload investment files
    - Zerodha / Coin
        - Navigate to : Coin app home --> Account --> Console --> Reports --> Click on three lines on top righ corner
            - Holdings File: Portfolio --> Holdings --> Download XLSX option available at the bottom right corner in Holdings tab
            - Trades File: Reports --> Tradebook --> Click on date range strip --> select segment as Mutual funds or any other type where your assets are present, leave symbol empty, Select date range at least 2months or the range that covers your resent all set of sips or trades --> Next button --> Download XLSX option available at the top right corner
